1.  **ANALYSIS:** This question asks whether a taxpayer's spouse died in either of the two years immediately preceding the taxable year.

2.  **PREDICATE SELECTION:** `spouse_died/2`

3.  **ARGUMENT IDENTIFICATION:**
    *   `Taxpayer`:  (Variable)
    *   `YearOfDeath`: (Variable)


4.  **QUERY:**
```prolog
case_query(Result) :-
    spouse_died(Taxpayer, YearOfDeath),
    ( YearOfDeath =:= TaxableYear - 1 ; YearOfDeath =:= TaxableYear -2 -> Result = true ; Result = false ).
```
